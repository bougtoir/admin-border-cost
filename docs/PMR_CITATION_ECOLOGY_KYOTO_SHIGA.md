# PMR Citation Ecology — Kyoto–Shiga (literature map)

Purpose: a PMR/SI-specific reading map for the retargeted paper. This is a
map of conversations to engage, not a padding list — every entry names what
it supplies to the argument. Candidate references; final list to be fixed at
manuscript stage against PMR style.

## 1. Place-based strategy and policy

- Barca, F. (2009), *An Agenda for a Reformed Cohesion Policy* — the
  foundational "place-based" argument: territorial context as the unit of
  policy design rather than uniform national instruments. Frames why a
  boundary is a design choice, not a default.
- Barca, McCann & Rodríguez-Pose (2012), "The case for regional development
  intervention: place-based versus place-neutral approaches", *Journal of
  Regional Science* — the place-based/place-neutral axis the paper's
  inside-out/outside-in wedge prices.
- Tomaney (2010), *Place-Based Approaches to Regional Development* —
  critical appraisal of place-based claims; guards against overclaiming
  territorial redesign benefits.

## 2. Public-service ecosystems and public service logic

- Osborne, S.P. (2018/2020), "From public service-dominant logic to public
  service logic", *Public Management Review* — value creation inside
  service ecosystems; supports "one territory, multiple functional
  geographies" by decoupling service systems from organizational
  containers.
- Petrescu, M. (2019), "From marketing to public value", *Public Management
  Review* — public value operationalization in service settings.
- Vargo & Lusch (2016), "Institutions and axioms", *Journal of the Academy
  of Marketing Science* — ecosystem/institution substrate for nested
  service geographies.
- Eriksson (2019), "Representative and deliberative configurations of
  democratic governance in public administration", *Public Administration
  Review* (PMR-adjacent) — legitimacy of governance across organizational
  vs service boundaries.

## 3. Public value creation and destruction

- Moore (1995), *Creating Public Value* — baseline value framework.
- Benington & Moore (eds, 2011), *Public Value: Theory and Practice*.
- Cui & Osborne (2023), "Unpacking public value creation and destruction",
  *Public Management Review* — value destruction as a measurable
  possibility; supports the opportunity-cost estimand reading.

## 4. Collaborative governance, intermunicipal and shared-service provision

- Ansell & Gash (2008), "Collaborative governance in theory and practice",
  *JPART* — canonical collaborative-governance model for services that
  cross administrative lines.
- Hulst & van Montfort (2007), "Inter-municipal cooperation: a widespread
  phenomenon", in Hulst & van Montfort (eds), *Inter-Municipal Cooperation
  in Europe* — municipal-scale pooling of functions across boundaries.
- Bel & Warner (2015), "Inter-municipal cooperation and costs", *Public
  Administration Review* — evidence that cooperation across boundaries is
  not self-justifying; cost effects are context-dependent. Pairs with the
  paper's "alignment is not self-justifying" finding.
- Emerson, Nabatchi & Balogh (2012), "An integrative framework for
  collaborative governance", *JPART* — meta-governance framing.

## 5. Territorial rescaling, functional regions, organizational vs service boundaries

- Brenner (2004), *New State Spaces* — state spatial restructuring;
  territory as produced, not given.
- Karlsson & Olsson (2006), "The identification of functional regions",
  *Papers in Regional Science* — travel-to-work functional regions; the
  functional geography against which the administrative container is
  benchmarked.
- OECD (2002/2012), functional urban areas / *Redefining "Urban"* —
  commuting-defined functional regions; already in the canonical
  manuscript's reference set.
- Agnew (1994); Newman & Paasi (1998); Paasi (2009); Painter (2010);
  Elden (2013) — the boundary-as-institution line, carried over from the
  validated manuscript; supports "inherited container" vocabulary.

## 6. Districting / spatial optimization methods (methods only, light touch)

- Ricca, Scozzari & Simeone (2013); Mehrotra et al. (1998); DeFord et al.
  (2021); Openshaw & Taylor (1979) — paired-optimization and MAUP machinery
  already cited in the canonical manuscript; retain only as much as the
  methods summary needs.

## 7. Explicitly excluded

No citations to shrinking-region service-provision literature that carried
the prior draft's contaminated examples (1-km demand grids, minimum
functional guarantees, mobile/digital substitution results). If the
healthcare/education extension proceeds, its own pre-registered provenance
and literature list will be built in `docs/PMR_KYOTO_SHIGA_NEW_DATA_PROVENANCE.md`
