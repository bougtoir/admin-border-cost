## 2. What the validated Kyoto–Shiga evidence actually supports

The canonical study (admin_border_cost) demonstrates, for ONE function —
electoral representation:

- An inherited administrative boundary can be made a measurable
  counterfactual constraint: Cost_border = L*(Boundary ON) − L*(Boundary
  OFF), solved as matched paired optimizations at two spatial resolutions
  (55 municipal/ward units; 11,978 census small areas).
- Visible functional entanglement does not automatically imply a high
  boundary cost: Kyoto–Shiga carries 79,466 daily cross-prefecture trips,
  the prefectural offices are the closest pair in Japan (~10.5 km, rank 1
  of 1,081), yet the measured cost is bounded [0, 532,293] at municipality
  scale (FEASIBLE, not OPTIMAL) and zero among best-found small-area maps;
  the real border crosses fewer trips (39,733) than all 200 matched
  placebo borders and sits in the cheapest feasible decile.
- Therefore — as a *theoretical hypothesis*, not a proven result — the same
  administrative boundary may carry different opportunity costs for
  different public functions.

## 3. Fit against the SI themes

| SI theme | Fit with Kyoto–Shiga contribution |
|---|---|
| Place-based strategy design | Strong: the paper treats the territorial container itself as a design variable, not background. |
| Locus / nested public-service ecosystems | Strong: one administrative territory (the prefecture pair) contains multiple functional geographies — electoral districts nested inside prefectures; commuting fields crossing them. |
| Inside-out vs outside-in strategy | Direct: Boundary ON is the inside-out (inherited container) design; Boundary OFF is the outside-in (functional geography) benchmark; the cost is the wedge between them. |
| Public value creation/destruction | Direct: misaligned containers are hypothesized value destroyers; the measured value of congruence must be priced, not presumed. Here the measurable wedge was small. |
| Territorial congruence as a design variable | Core contribution: "territorial congruence is a design choice to be evaluated function by function, not a default to be inherited." |
| Robustness/reconfiguration under demographic/fiscal pressure | Partial: validated study includes IPSS 2050 robustness and a minimal-intervention (M5) reconfiguration benchmark; additional service functions are only planned. |

## 4. Explicit weakness

The validated evidence covers **electoral representation only**. The
signature claim — different functions may optimally carry different
territories — is a hypothesis disciplined by one worked example, not an
empirical comparison of service systems. The abstract must keep
COMPLETED EVIDENCE and PLANNED EXTENSION strictly separate.

## 5. Judgment

**A — a conceptual paper with the Kyoto–Shiga worked example is
sufficient** for a competitive abstract under this call (which explicitly
welcomes conceptual papers), provided the option for up to two additional
function-specific analyses is kept open as a pre-specified extension
(`docs/PMR_KYOTO_SHIGA_FUNCTION_SELECTION_FREEZE.md`,
`docs/PMR_KYOTO_SHIGA_ANALYSIS_FREEZE.md`). If guest editors admit late
entry and favor stronger empirics, those two analyses are the intended
strengthening — they are planned, not completed, and must never be
written as findings.
