## 2. Rules to prevent overlap

- The PMR paper summarizes the electoral evidence in at most a
  worked-example section: estimand definition, headline bounds
  ([0, 532,293] Level A; best-found 0 Level B; 39,733 cross-flow vs 200
  placebos), and the honest precision caveats. It must NOT reproduce the
  electoral paper's full model suite, tables, figures, or sensitivity
  apparatus.
- Reuse of prose: no paragraph may be copied verbatim between the two
  manuscripts; the estimand may be restated in PMR vocabulary
  ("alignment opportunity cost of an inherited container").
- The electoral manuscript is cited as the source of the worked example
  (self-citation, declared), satisfying PMR's empirical anchor without
  duplicating it.
- New Kyoto–Shiga analyses (healthcare, education — if run) belong to
  the PMR paper only and are new contributions, not recycled content.
- Framing discipline: no shrinking-region service-provision residue
  (denylist, `docs/PMR_CONTAMINATION_ROOT_CAUSE.md`); no claim that the
  electoral paper estimated service systems.

## 3. Audit result

With these boundaries, overlap is limited to: (a) the same case
geography (unavoidable and disclosed); (b) a summarized worked example
(cited). The PMR paper's claim, theory, and any new empirics are
independent of the electoral paper's contributions.
